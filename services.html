Bookkeeping & Corporate Accounting

We deliver the full spectrum of accounting functions — starting with clean, consistent foundational bookkeeping and scaling all the way up to high-level statutory compliance under International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) for SMEs and South African corporate regulations.

The Accounting Spectrum: From basic day-to-day cashbook reconciliations to audited annual financial statements, we structure our support to meet your exact organizational stage.
  • Entry-Level Bookkeeping & Cashbook Recons: Daily and monthly reconciliations, bank matching, and cash flow tracking
  • General Ledger Maintenance: Accurate recording of accounts receivable, payable, and fixed asset registers
  • Monthly Management Accounts: Clear, actionable periodic reporting for executive financial discipline
  • Budgeting & Forecasting: Strategic variance analysis and predictive liquidity planning
  • High-Level Compliance & AFS: Comprehensive Annual Financial Statement preparation aligned with IFRS and CIPC governance

Tax Consulting & SARS Compliance

Strategic tax planning and strict adherence to the South African Revenue Service (SARS) requirements to minimise liabilities and avoid penalties. We help you stay ahead of filing deadlines and legislative changes.

  • Corporate income tax (IT14) and provisional tax returns
  • Value-Added Tax (VAT) registrations and bi-monthly submissions
  • Individual tax returns for company directors and high-net-worth individuals
  • Tax clearance certificates and dispute resolution with SARS
  • Capital gains tax and dividends tax advisory
  • Tax structuring for trusts and corporate groups

Payroll Administration & HR Services

Confidential and accurate payroll processing tailored for businesses operating in Gauteng. We manage the full employee remuneration lifecycle so you can focus on growing your team.

  • PAYE, UIF, and SDL registrations and monthly EMP201 submissions
  • Bi-annual EMP501 reconciliations and IRP5 generation
  • Workmen's Compensation (COIDA) annual returns and letters of good standing
  • Employee contracts and payslip distribution
  • Leave and benefits administration
  • Payroll audits and SARS payroll health checks

CIPC & Secretarial Services

Maintaining legal entity compliance with the Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC). We ensure your company records are accurate, current and filed on time.

  • New company registrations and name reservations
  • Annual return submissions and director amendments
  • Share certificate issuance and beneficial ownership declarations
  • Company profile requests and status checks
  • Amendments to memoranda of incorporation (MOI)
  • Registered office and statutory record maintenance
