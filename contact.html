FAQ

Answers before you reach out

Practical details on response times, meetings, fees and how we handle your enquiry — so you can contact us with confidence.

Send an enquiry
01 How quickly will you respond to my enquiry?

We typically respond within one business day. For time-sensitive SARS, payroll or CIPC matters, note your deadline in the message and email info@powerfin.co.za so we can prioritise appropriately.

02 Do you only work with businesses in Pretoria and Johannesburg?

Our practice is based in Garsfontein and primarily serves businesses across Pretoria and Johannesburg. We also support Gauteng clients remotely where the engagement allows. Tell us where you operate when you get in touch.

03 What should I include when I contact you?

Share your name, business name, preferred contact details, the service you need (accounting, tax, payroll or CIPC), and a short summary of your situation. A phone number is optional if you would like a callback.

04 Can we meet in person at your Garsfontein office?

Yes. Meetings at our registered office at 588 Dalmation Street, Garsfontein can be arranged by appointment. Email us or use the enquiry form to request a suitable time.

05 How do fees and engagements work?

Fees depend on scope and may be fixed, retainer-based or time-and-materials. After your enquiry we clarify requirements and share a clear fee basis before substantive work begins. See our Terms of Service for general engagement conditions.

06 Is my personal information protected under POPIA?

Yes. Enquiries and client information are handled in line with POPIA and our Privacy Policy. We do not sell personal information. You can review your rights and our processing practices on that page.

07 What services can I enquire about?

You can enquire about bookkeeping & corporate accounting, tax consulting & SARS compliance, payroll administration, and CIPC secretarial services. Browse Services for full detail, then contact us with your priorities.
