This Privacy Policy should be read together with our Terms of Service. Where you engage us for professional services, our client engagement letter or agreement will also apply and may contain additional confidentiality and data-protection terms.

1. Introduction

POWERFIN ACCOUNTANTS (“PowerFin”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is committed to protecting personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (“POPIA”), the Promotion of Access to Information Act 2 of 2000 (“PAIA”) where applicable, and other relevant South African law.

We process personal information lawfully, reasonably and transparently, only for identified purposes related to our professional practice and this website.

2. Responsible Party

For the purposes of POPIA, PowerFin Accountants is the responsible party in respect of personal information collected through this website and in the ordinary course of providing accounting, tax, payroll and secretarial services.

  • Legal name: POWERFIN ACCOUNTANTS (private company, in business)
  • Legal address: 588 Dalmation Street, Garsfontein, Gauteng, 0042 (opens in a new tab)
  • Mailing address: P O Box 927, Garsfontein, Gauteng, 0042
  • Email: info@powerfin.co.za
  • Areas served: Pretoria and Johannesburg, Gauteng

Requests relating to personal information should be directed to the contact details in section 19. Where we appoint or update an Information Officer or Deputy Information Officer as required under POPIA, those details will be published here or provided on request.

3. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • Visitors to www.powerfin.co.za and related pages
  • Individuals who submit enquiries via our contact form or email
  • Clients, prospective clients, and their directors, officers, employees, shareholders and other representatives whose information we process while delivering services
  • Suppliers and other business contacts, where relevant

It does not apply to third-party websites linked from our site. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

4. Information We Collect

Depending on your interaction with us, we may collect the following categories of personal information:

4.1 Contact and identity

  • Full name, title, job role and preferred contact details
  • Email address, telephone number and physical or postal address
  • Identity or passport numbers, where required for tax, payroll, CIPC or FICA-related obligations

4.2 Business and entity information

  • Company or trading name, registration number, VAT number and tax reference numbers
  • Industry, structure, beneficial ownership and director particulars
  • Banking details for fee payments or client disbursements, where necessary

4.3 Professional and financial information

  • Accounting records, ledgers, invoices, bank statements and supporting documents
  • Tax returns, SARS correspondence, assessments and related filings
  • Payroll data (including remuneration, deductions, leave and statutory contributions)
  • CIPC and secretarial records, share registers and related company documents

4.4 Website and communication data

  • Information submitted through our contact form (including message content and consent preferences)
  • Standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information and pages visited, via server logs where collected

5. How We Collect Information

We collect personal information:

  • Directly from you (website forms, email, telephone, meetings and document uploads)
  • From your authorised representatives, directors or employees
  • From third parties where lawfully permitted (for example SARS eFiling portals, CIPC, banks or previous accountants, with your authority)
  • Automatically through ordinary website hosting and security logs

Where you provide personal information of another person (for example an employee for payroll), you confirm that you are authorised to do so and that the individual has been informed of this policy where required.

6. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information only for lawful purposes related to our practice, including to:

  • Respond to enquiries and schedule consultations
  • Assess whether we can accept an engagement and prepare proposals or engagement letters
  • Provide bookkeeping, accounting, tax, payroll, CIPC and related professional services
  • Prepare and lodge submissions with SARS, CIPC, the Department of Employment and Labour, and other regulators where instructed
  • Manage billing, payments, credit control and practice administration
  • Communicate deadlines, filing requirements, service updates and practice notices
  • Maintain quality control, training, risk management and professional indemnity processes
  • Comply with legal, regulatory and professional obligations
  • Secure, monitor and improve our website and IT systems

We do not sell personal information.

7. Lawful Basis for Processing

Under POPIA, we process personal information on one or more of the following bases:

  • Consent — for example, where you tick the consent box on our contact form, or where consent is otherwise required
  • Contract — to conclude or perform an engagement agreement with you or your organisation
  • Legal obligation — to comply with South African law (including tax, companies, labour and record-keeping requirements)
  • Legitimate interest — to operate, secure and develop our professional practice in a way that does not override your rights

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal will not affect processing already carried out lawfully, and may affect our ability to continue certain services.

8. Special Personal Information

In limited circumstances we may process special personal information (as defined in POPIA), for example health-related information relevant to payroll or statutory benefits, or biometric identifiers if ever used for identity verification. We will only process such information where permitted by POPIA (including with consent, or where necessary for a legal duty or other authorised ground) and with appropriate safeguards.

We do not intentionally collect special personal information through the public website contact form. Please do not submit sensitive health, biometric or similar data via the website unless we specifically request it through a secure channel.

9. Operators and Sharing of Information

We may share personal information with:

  • Operators who process information on our behalf (for example IT hosting providers, secure document portals, accounting or payroll software vendors, email and cloud services), under confidentiality and security obligations consistent with POPIA
  • Regulators and authorities such as SARS, CIPC, the Information Regulator, courts or law-enforcement agencies, where required or authorised by law
  • Professional advisers such as attorneys, insurers or auditors engaged by us, where necessary for legal or risk purposes
  • Other parties where you instruct us to do so, or where disclosure is necessary to protect rights, safety or property

We require operators to process personal information only for agreed purposes and to implement appropriate security measures.

10. Cross-Border Transfers

Some operators or systems we use may store or process information outside the Republic of South Africa. Where personal information is transferred outside South Africa, we will take reasonable steps to ensure that the recipient is subject to a law, binding corporate rules or binding agreement that provides an adequate level of protection aligned with POPIA section 72, or that another lawful ground for transfer applies.

11. Security Safeguards

We implement appropriate, reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, damage, unauthorised access and unlawful processing. Measures typically include:

  • Access controls and role-based permissions
  • Encrypted communication channels where technically feasible
  • Secure storage, backup and device-security practices
  • Confidentiality obligations for staff and contractors
  • POPIA awareness and need-to-know handling of client data
  • Incident response processes for suspected security compromises

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If we become aware of a security compromise affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as required by POPIA.

12. Retention of Information

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes collected, or as required by law or professional standards. Typical retention considerations include:

  • Tax and related records — generally for periods required under the Tax Administration Act and related legislation (often five years or longer from the relevant assessment or submission date)
  • Company and secretarial records — as required by the Companies Act and related regulations
  • Payroll and employment-related records — as required by labour and tax laws
  • Enquiry and website correspondence — for a reasonable period to manage follow-up and practice administration

When information is no longer required, we securely destroy, delete or de-identify it, subject to any legal hold or dispute.

13. Your Rights Under POPIA

Subject to lawful limitations, you have the right to:

  • Be notified that personal information about you is being collected
  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction, destruction or deletion of personal information that is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, out of date, incomplete, misleading or unlawfully obtained
  • Object to processing on reasonable grounds relating to your particular situation, or for direct marketing
  • Request restriction of processing in appropriate circumstances
  • Withdraw consent where processing is consent-based
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in section 19. We may need to verify your identity before responding. We will respond within a reasonable time and in line with POPIA and, where relevant, PAIA procedures.

Please note that we may decline certain requests where we are legally required or entitled to retain or continue processing information (for example statutory tax records or information needed for an ongoing engagement or dispute).

14. Direct Marketing

We may send service-related communications that are necessary to perform our engagement (for example filing reminders). Where we send electronic marketing communications, we will do so in accordance with POPIA and the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002, including obtaining consent where required and honouring opt-out requests.

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time by following the instructions in the message or contacting info@powerfin.co.za.

15. Cookies and Website Analytics

This website does not currently use non-essential tracking cookies or third-party analytics. Essential technical logs may be generated by our hosting environment for security and performance. If we introduce cookies or analytics in future, we will update this policy and obtain consent where required by law.

16. Children

Our website and professional services are directed at businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18 through this website. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can delete it where appropriate.

17. Complaints

If you are dissatisfied with how we handle your personal information, please contact us first so we can attempt to resolve the matter. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa:

  • Website: inforegulator.org.za (opens in a new tab)
  • Email (complaints): POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za
  • Email (general enquiries): enquiries@inforegulator.org.za

18. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology or legal requirements. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective or last-updated date. Continued use of the website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy, to the extent permitted by law. Material changes affecting clients may also be communicated through our usual engagement channels.
