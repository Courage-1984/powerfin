POPIA Compliance Statement

Powerfin Accountants is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). We act as a responsible party in respect of personal information collected through this website and our client engagement processes.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Contact details (name, email address, phone number)
  • Business information (company name, registration number, industry)
  • Financial and accounting records provided during service delivery
  • Website usage data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) via standard server logs

2. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information only for lawful purposes directly related to our services, including:

  • Responding to enquiries submitted through the contact form
  • Providing accounting, tax, payroll and secretarial services
  • Complying with SARS, CIPC and other statutory obligations
  • Communicating important updates, deadlines and service-related information
  • Maintaining accurate client records and internal quality controls

3. Lawful Basis for Processing

We process personal information on the basis of:

  • Consent, where expressly provided (for example, ticking the consent box on our contact form)
  • Contractual necessity, to perform our engagement agreement with you
  • Legal obligation, to comply with applicable South African laws and regulations
  • Legitimate interest, to operate and secure our practice effectively

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to safeguard personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. These measures include:

  • Access controls and role-based permissions
  • Encrypted communication channels where technically feasible
  • Secure storage systems and regular backups
  • POPIA awareness training for our team
  • Confidentiality clauses in all employment and contractor agreements

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We may share information only:

  • With trusted third-party service providers who assist us in delivering services, under strict confidentiality obligations
  • Where required by law, regulation or legal process (for example, SARS or CIPC submissions)
  • To protect our rights, property or safety, or that of our clients

6. Your Rights Under POPIA

As a data subject, you have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction or deletion of inaccurate or outdated information
  • Object to the processing of your personal information in certain circumstances
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa

7. Retention of Information

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law. Once no longer required, information is securely destroyed or de-identified.

8. Cookies and Website Analytics

This website does not currently use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. If this changes in the future, we will update this policy and obtain consent where required by law.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
