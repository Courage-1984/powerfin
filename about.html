Our Story

Why PowerFin Exists

PowerFin Accountants was established to serve the economic heartbeat of Gauteng — the entrepreneurs, SMEs, schools, trusts and corporate entities that keep Pretoria and Johannesburg moving.

We saw too many businesses wrestling with reactive compliance: missed deadlines, unclear reports and the anxiety of SARS correspondence. Our mission is to replace that anxiety with structured, proactive financial management.
