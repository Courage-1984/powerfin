## **Logo Analysis & Design Reasoning**

The provided logo features a distinct mix of styles. "PowerFin" uses a custom, slightly stylised script/serif blend, while "Accountants" and "Beyond accuracy" use a very clean, geometric sans-serif. The primary visual anchors are the dual forward-facing arrows, which use a gradient transitioning from a medium blue to a darker navy.  
To build a minimalist web interface (Static HTML, CSS, Vanilla JS) that feels modern and fast, we will lean into the clean, geometric sans-serif used in the sub-text, paired with the strong blues, rather than replicating the complex script font for standard web text.

## **Brand Identity: Colours & Typography**

### **Colour Palette**

> * **Primary Blue (PowerFin Blue):** \#144E96 — Extracted from the lighter portion of the logo arrows. Use this for main headings, primary CTA buttons, and active link states.  
> * **Deep Navy (Shadow Accent):** \#0B2A54 — Extracted from the darker shadows/edges of the logo. Use for footer backgrounds, secondary typography, and hover states.  
> * **Graphite Black:** \#1A1A1A — Extracted from the "Accountants" text. A softer alternative to pure black for primary body text to reduce eye strain.  
> * **Crisp White:** \#FFFFFF — Main website background.  
> * **Soft Grey (Background Accent):** \#F7FAFC — Used for alternating section backgrounds (e.g., separating the "About" section from the "Services" grid) without breaking a minimalist aesthetic.

### **Typography Pairing**

> * **Headings:** Montserrat or Poppins. Both are geometric, modern, and highly legible, perfectly mirroring the clean sans-serif typography used in the "Accountants" portion of the logo.  
> * **Body Text:** Inter or Open Sans. Neutral, highly readable web-safe fonts that pair well with geometric headings without competing for visual attention.
